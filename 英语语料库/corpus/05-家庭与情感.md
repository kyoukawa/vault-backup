# 家庭与情感 2026.11.9

> 适用：家庭主题 / 作品展介绍 / 亲情 / 代际沟通 / 科技与家庭
> 来源：海淀区期中「爱在我家」作品展（4 篇范文，含 18 分卷）
> 核心：**一个小场景 + 两三条理由 + 一句升华**

---

## 一、审题三条（这题的命门）

| # | 要求 |
|---|---|
| 1 | **`Loving my family` 是题眼**：作品必须紧扣家庭之爱，不能写成摄影/手工技巧分享 |
| 2 | **详写「为什么选它」**，不是流水账介绍内容 |
| 3 | 好作品三个标准：`specific details` 有细节 / `concrete, not vague` 不空洞 / `touching` 有感染力 |

---

## 二、具体事例

| What | How | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| a New Year's dinner photo | every family member around the table, chatter and laughter filling the room | capture a moment no one had to arrange | real family love needs no presentation — it is ubiquitous |
| porcelain figurines of daily life | figures of the family petting the cat, reading a story, knitting sweaters | freeze ordinary moments into something permanent | small things carry the heaviest emotional weight |
| decorating the home for the Spring Festival（分工场景 ★ 最值钱） | mum and grandma making dumplings, dad sweeping the ceiling, my sister and I putting up paper-cuttings | show everyone working side by side with smiles on their faces | family love lives in ordinary moments, not just festivals |

**推荐表达：**

> `What I submitted was a photo taken while all my family members were gathering around ...`

> `These figures are small in size, but weigh a lot emotionally.`

> `Although everyone had a different task, we worked together with smiles on our faces, making our home feel festive and welcoming.`
> （**分工 + 共同**是这组句子的骨架：`Although everyone had a different task, we ... together`）

---

## 三、通用理由

| # | 推荐表达 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `tight-knit emotional bonds in the family` | 家庭中紧密的情感纽带 |
| 2 | `the warm, caring bonds between family members` | 家人间温暖关怀的纽带 |
| 3 | `helping every family member keep up with the fast-changing world and ensuring no one gets left behind` | 帮每个家人跟上变化的世界，不让任何人被落下 |
| 4 | `love is more than caring for one another amid the small routines of everyday life` | 爱不只是日常琐事中的相互照顾 |
| 5 | `The profound meaning of family love lies in ...` | 亲情的深刻意义在于…… |
| 6 | `It reminds us that love at home consists in small daily interactions.` | 它提醒我们，家里的爱存在于日常点滴 |
| 7 | `love is not limited to special occasions or grand gestures` | 爱不限于特殊节日或盛大举动 ★★★ |
| 8 | `what truly matters is how we treat each other in daily life` | 真正重要的是我们在日常中如何对待彼此 ★★★ |
| 9 | `love is not measured by what you say or buy, but by how you treat your family` | 爱不由说了什么、买了什么衡量，而由你如何对待家人衡量 ★★ |

---

## 四、通用句式

| 功能 | 推荐表达 |
|---|---|
| 开场入题 | `What I submitted was a photo taken while ...` |
| 说明选择 | `I finally decided to submit this picture since it fully embodies ...` |
| 转折升华 | `These pictures taught me that ... doesn't need to be presented — it is ubiquitous.` |
| 结论收束 | `It reminds us that love at home consists in small daily interactions.` |

---

## 五、考场调用

1. **家庭题**：选一个具体场景（年夜饭 / 教长辈用手机）→ 两条理由（情感纽带 + 日常点滴）→ 收在 `consists in small daily interactions`
2. **科技与家庭交叉**：`helping ... keep up with the fast-changing world` + `ensuring no one gets left behind`，直接扣"不让任何人被技术落下"
3. **避免**：写成摄影技巧说明；只喊 `I love my family` 而无具体动作

---

## 六、素材池

用户未选：`cherish each other, support each other`（与理由 1/2 重复）／`technology is invisibly distancing family members`（反向论证暂不用）／教爷爷手机支付、父母捧证书（具体事例暂不单独收）

## 七、规避清单（来自 7.5 分卷的逐句批改 · 2026-11-9）

| 错误 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `It was a busy scene that we were decorating our home` | `It captures a busy scene in which we were decorating our home` | that 引导定语从句时从句不完整；改 in which |
| `prepare for the upcoming year` | `preparing for the coming year` | 与主句时态不搭；coming 比 upcoming 更自然 |
| `with smile on our face` | `with smiles on our faces` | 可数名词单数裸用；复数一致 |
| `love not just exist` | `love does not exist only` / `love is not limited to` | 否定词位置错误 + 主谓一致 |
| `love is not determined by what you say or buy, but showed by ...` | `love is not measured by what you say or buy, but by how you treat your family` | 并列结构不对称；determine→measure 更地道 |
| 理由段反复解释「爱不只在节日」 | 只说一遍，把篇幅让给**具体动作** | 老师原话：因原因部分重复，建议压缩议论 |

**老师给的三条二稿建议（通用性极高）：**
1. **保留具体场景** —— 人物分工是全文最有价值的部分
2. **减少观点重复** —— 围绕一个观点展开，不必反复解释
3. **加强细节与感悟的联系** —— 从"一起装饰房屋"自然过渡到"相互帮助、共同营造温暖"
