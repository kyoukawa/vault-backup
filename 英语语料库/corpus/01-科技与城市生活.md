# 科技与 AI 2026.11.4

> 适用：科技节邀请 / 推荐科技城市 / 未来生活 / **单独考 AI**
> 这一份是「纯 AI」题的独立弹药库，不依赖文化或健康语境

---

## 一、AI 通用四句（纯 AI 题专用）

| # | 推荐表达 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `AI works best when it quietly weaves itself into our daily lives.` | 主题句，`weave` 是画面动词 |
| 2 | `It doesn't replace human warmth — it handles the repetitive tasks so we can focus on what matters.` | 破折号转折，论证"不是冷冰冰" |
| 3 | `Technology is redefining how we live, learn and connect with others.` | 三动词并列，万能主题句 |
| 4 | `AI may be powerful, but it is only a tool — the choice is still ours.` | 收尾留思考，不空洞 |

---

## 二、扣「未来已来」的三种写法

| 方法 | 表达 |
|---|---|
| 从"展示科技"到"让未来可感知" | `Visitors can experience technologies that once seemed futuristic.` |
| 从"科技很先进"到"重构生活方式" | `Technology is redefining how we live, learn and connect.` |
| 用未来场景扣题 | `smart cities / smart homes / autonomous transport / AI assistants` |

---

## 三、AI 事例（跨题复用）

| What | How | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| driverless robotaxis | call one with your phone, it avoids obstacles and adjusts speed on its own | make short trips safe and effortless | a real taste of future mobility |
| AI lab / tech company visit | try out 5G smart home devices, generate a painting with AI, code drones to fly | experience technology firsthand | technology is no longer something you only read about |
| AI learning assistant | analyse your weaknesses and tailor exercises; instant feedback | learn at your own pace | turn frustration into confidence |
| service robots in malls | robotic arms make coffee, human-shaped robots give directions | make shopping faster and more fun | convenience woven into daily life |

---

## 四、考场调用

1. **纯 AI**：四句里挑 #1 + #2，中间插一个具体事例，收尾 #4
2. **AI + 某个领域**：把领域词塞进 #3 → `redefining how we learn / how we stay healthy / how we enjoy culture`
3. **避免的坑**：只列产品名（robot、5G、VR）而不写"人用它做了什么"，等于没写
