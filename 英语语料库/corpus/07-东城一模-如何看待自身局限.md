# 东城一模·如何看待作者处理自身局限 2026.11.24

> 适用：英语阅表/读后续写「评价人物应对方式」类
> 来源：2025–2026 东城一模「How do you think of the way the author deals with his limitations?」（约 40 词）课堂复习资料实拍
> 定位：**阅表题实战范例**，配套 [[06-阅表题写作思路]]（五层价值转换）使用
> 说明：英文素材据实拍 OCR 整理；中文词块为可读部分整理，个别需对照原图

---

## 一、审题要点（决定成败）

| 关键词 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| what do you think | 必须表达**个人评价/观点**，用第一人称 `I think / I believe`，不能只复述作者经历 |
| the way | 评价的是**处理方式**（怎么应对），不是结果 |
| his limitations | 指作者的**口吃**及由此带来的表达困难、自我形象受损等 |
| 立场 | 不是消除局限，而是**接受局限、欢迎新视角、以适当努力继续参与** |

**核心判据**：观点新 + 落到个人启示。这是阅表题和纯复述的分水岭。

---

## 二、答题逻辑链：P-E-E-L 结构

可参考 PEEL 结构：**Point（观点）→ Evidence（证据）→ Elaboration（阐释）→ Link（启示）**

```
Point        I think his way is wise and helpful.
Evidence     He accepts his limitation instead of hiding it and keeps participating with proper effort.
Elaboration  This is mature because he focuses on what he can control.
Link         It reminds me to embrace my imperfections and keep trying.
```

**这正对应「五种价值转换角度」的用法**：Evidence 停事实层，Elaboration 升认知层（mature / focus on what he can control），Link 落成长层个人启示（reminds me to ...）。

---

## 三、词块积累（考场直接调用）

### 评价作者方式
- `be wise and helpful` 明智而有助益
- `be inspiring / positive / constructive / realistic / mature` 鼓舞人心/积极/有建设性/现实/成熟
- `show real maturity` 体现真正的成熟
- `a good lesson about how to cope with shortcomings` 应对自身缺点的好教训

### 描述接纳局限
- `come to terms with his limitations` 与自己的局限和解
- `accept one's limitations` 接受自己的局限
- `confront one's weaknesses bravely` 勇敢面对自己的弱点
- `instead of running away from / covering up` 而不是逃避/掩盖
- `lessen their influence` 减轻它们的影响
- `welcome new perspectives` 欢迎新视角
- `let the stutter go` 对口吃顺其自然
- `participate in any conversation` 参与任何对话

### 写个人启示
- `embrace my imperfections` 拥抱自己的不完美
- `focus on what I can do` 专注于我能做的事
- `not let limitations define me` 不让局限定义我
- `turn a burden into a benefit` 把负担转化为益处
- `keep striving for self-improvement` 坚持自我提升

---

## 四、立意方向（三条主线，选一）

1. **成熟在接纳**：`real maturity lies in accepting what we cannot change and focusing on what we can do`
2. **转负为正**：`limitations, handled wisely, can make us stronger`——把局限当力量而非负担
3. **行动不放弃**：接纳 ≠ 被动，`accepting limitations while actively seeking treatment/strategies`

---

## 五、学生作品选（同题异写，看差距）

- **A17（65 词）**：立场稳 `I think this is wise`，落点 `embrace and make the most of them rather than eliminating them`，进阶 `inner peace, and even confidence`——三层推进，典范。
- **住宿部（74 词）**：对比写法 `Rather than pouring tremendous energy into covering up his weaknesses, a practice that would prove time-consuming and futile, he chose to devote himself to acquiring knowledge`——反证有力，落 `nobody is perfect, yet we can keep striving for self-improvement`。
- **另一学生（51 词）**：`bravely accepted his imperfection while trying to live a normal life`，落 `turning my burden into a benefit`。

**可学习的共性**：都先给**明确立场句**（wise/positive/mature），中间用**对比**（rather than ... he chose ...）撑起论证，结尾落**个人启示**（reminds me to ... / I have realized ...）。没有一篇是单纯复述作者经历的。

---

## 六、考场调用

1. 看到「how do you think of the way sb deals with ...」→ 第一句必须表个人立场
2. 用 P-E-E-L 四步推进，避免停在事实层
3. 词块从「评价/接纳/启示」三类里各挑一两个，不要堆砌
4. 收尾落个人启示，用 `reminds me to ...` / `I have realized ...`（对应五层里的成长层/人生层）
