# 文化与传承 2026.11.4

> 适用：传统文化节 / 文化自信 / 介绍中国文化 / 文化遗产 / 文化×AI
> **不做北京景点题**，只做通用文化框架
> 来源：东城期末「传统文化与文化自信」+「Photo Beijing」可迁移部分

---

## 一、三层立意（考场的递进骨架）

| 层 | 讲什么 | 关键词 |
|---|---|---|
| 形式之潮 | 用年轻人喜欢的形式呈现传统 | 汉服走秀、说唱、皮影新编 |
| 传播之潮 | 人人可参与，不再只是"师徒传授" | 门槛降低、共同创造 |
| 精神之潮 | 文化自信，年轻一代对自身文化的认同 | 当代语言讲永恒故事 |

**公式**：`传统内核 + 现代创意 = 文化新生`

---

## 二、文化×AI（最值钱的一组）

| What | How | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| AR calligraphy | brush strokes transform into animated landscapes on screen | make calligraphy come alive | technology can serve as a powerful medium for cultural transmission |
| VR journey to the Song Dynasty | put on VR glasses and live a day as a Song citizen | make history something you can walk into | let the young meet the old face to face |
| an AI folk-music orchestra | AI musicians perform Chinese folk melodies | keep ancient tunes accessible | tradition finds new voices |
| a mechanical arm painting sugar pictures | melted sugar is turned into patterns by a robotic arm | pass on a folk craft in a new way | old skills survive in new hands |

**推荐表达：**

> Technology doesn't replace tradition — it gives it a new stage.

---

## 三、通用理由

| # | 推荐表达 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Tradition is not something frozen in the past, but a living force that evolves with us.` | 传统不是凝固在过去，而是与我们共同演进的力量 |
| 2 | `The ancient and the modern are not opponents but partners.` | 古与今不是对手而是伙伴 |
| 3 | `Only when the youth fall in love with tradition can it be truly passed down.` | 只有年轻人爱上传统，它才真正传承 |
| 4 | `a sense of cultural confidence welled up inside me` | 文化自信在心中涌起 |
| 5 | `it was not just an event; it was a declaration of identity` | 不只是活动，更是一种身份宣言 |
| 6 | `cultural heritage gains new vitality through creative expression` | 文化遗产通过创意表达获得新生 |

---

## 四、考场调用

1. **纯文化**：选一个文化活动 → 三层递进（形式→传播→精神）→ 收在文化自信
2. **文化×AI**：AR 书法 / VR 宋城 → `make ... come alive` → `technology serves as a medium for cultural transmission`
3. **收尾**：`Only when the youth fall in love with tradition can it be truly passed down.`
