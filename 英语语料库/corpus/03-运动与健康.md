# 运动与健康 2026.11.4

> 适用：校园运动会 / 健康倡议 / 给 WHO 提建议 / 运动习惯 / 健康×AI
> 来源：海淀二模「Together for Well-Being」+「When Sports Meet AI」

---

## 一、四维健康框架（考场直接选两条写）

| 维度 | 落地动作（可执行） |
|---|---|
| 身体健康 Physical | `make regular physical activity part of the daily routine — one hour a day` |
| 心理健康 Mental | `take regular breaks from study to avoid burnout`；`stay away from bad habits` |
| 社会健康 Social | `build supportive peer relationships and take part in group activities` |
| 数字健康 Digital | `manage smartphone use sensibly and look out for one another` |

**题目三大要求**：`concrete 可落地` / `not vague 不空洞` / `easy to adopt 易被采纳`

---

## 二、健康×AI（最该补的一块）

| What | How | Effect | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-powered motion correction | cameras capture arm swing and leg lift in real time | point out mistakes you can't notice yourself | make exercise safer and more effective |
| AI-personalized fitness plan | the app analyses your daily habits and tailors a plan to your fitness level | make exercise more science-based and personalized | bring a coach into everyone's pocket |
| AI heart-rate monitoring in a 400m race | sensors track heart rate and warn runners when it is too high | prevent overexertion | put safety before performance |
| AI jump-rope counting | a device counts jumps and corrects your posture | turn a dull drill into a game | make exercise more appealing |

**推荐表达：**

> AI makes exercise more science-based, safer and more personalized.
>
> It serves people instead of replacing them.

---

## 三、通用理由

| Effect | Value |
|---|---|
| relieve stress and maintain a positive mood | improve both body and mind |
| redirect screen time to friends and family | genuine connections exist in the real world, which in turn boosts mental well-being |
| develop a healthier lifestyle | it is never too late to start |

---

## 四、提建议三句式（必背）

| 形式 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| doing | `I suggest building regular exercise into our daily routine.` |
| that 从句 | `I recommend that the WHO launch a "World Health Week".` |
| advocate / appeal | `We advocate reducing screen time before bed.` / `We appeal to young people to get moving.` |

---

## 五、考场调用

1. **纯健康**：四维选二 → 每条一个落地动作 + 一个理由 → 结尾 `Together, we can take small steps towards a healthier future.`
2. **健康×AI**：AI 定制方案 + 动作矫正 → `more science-based and personalized` → `serves people instead of replacing them`
3. **体育精神**：`AI may surpass humans in strength and speed, but it can never replace the sportsmanship that defines us.`
