Dear Forum Members, Among the six proposed cultural materials--Peking Opera, the Pyramids, the Great Wall, China's high-speed
rail, sounds of nature, and "hello" in different languages--each represents unique aspects of human civilization. However, I strongly recommend sending "hello" in various languages to outer space.
This simple yet powerful word embodies humanity's core values of connection and friendship. By including greetings from every nation, we showcase Earth's beautiful diversity while emphasizing our shared desire for peaceful communication. Unlike physical monuments or technological achievements, spoken words directly convey human warmth and openness to interstellar dialogue.
Let us choose this universal message of goodwill to convey humanity's sincerest aspirations for communication with extraterrestrial life. Yours, Li Jin
